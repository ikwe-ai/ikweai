About

A person signs this work.

Ikwe.ai is the company behind the EQ Safety Benchmark. It exists because whether an AI was safe for the person in front of it is a clinical question that has to be answered at engineering scale, and almost nobody holds both fields. Ikwe was built to hold both, independently.

Who

The founder

Ikwe is founder-led by Stephanie Stranko, Founder and CEO. She built the EQSB by translating behaviors that clinical practice already requires of licensed humans into an instrument that can score them in a machine transcript at volume.

Who else

The team

Ikwe is founder-led and co-founder-led, with a chief of staff and a small team of under ten, an independent research team based in the United States with research partners in New Zealand and Canada, and a bench of advisors across clinical practice, insurance and law. Ikwe names roles rather than people here; the people are named in the room, not on a webpage.

Independence

How independence is structured

Ikwe is third-party by structure, not by claim. We do not sit inside any lab we measure. We are not on their payroll, not in their governance, and not steered by their commercial pressure. We charge for measurement, and only for measurement: no remediation business, no equity in the systems we score, no financial tie to any AI we measure, and no financial interest in the result. We would rather be checked than taken on faith.

Progress

Milestones

Selected for the Iowa Go-To-Market Accelerator, 2026 cohort. The EQSB public specification v1.0 published July 2026. Patent pending. Independent peer review of the methodology is underway, and a larger pre-registered study is in progress.
