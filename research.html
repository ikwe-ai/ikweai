What we found

We measure what others skip.

Most AI systems are graded on toxicity and jailbreaks. We measure how the interaction actually affects the person, the way a clinician would, across scenarios of distress, dependency, and risk. The findings below come from our formative studies — a pilot corpus the pre-registered flagship study now in progress will regenerate at benchmark scale.

The method

A gate, then eight dimensions.

The EQ Safety Benchmark scores a response in two stages: a binary safety gate for behaviors that cause harm, then eight clinical quality dimensions grounded in established trauma-informed literature. Independently reviewed methodology, peer review underway. The method is patent pending.

Open for research

The eight dimension names, the gate concept, the study findings, the released summaries, and the scenario categories. Enough to understand and cite the work.

Protected

The scoring weights, the full violation set, the behavioral markers, the scoring formula, and the scenario library. Trade secret and patent pending.
