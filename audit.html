Independent Behavioral Evaluation Under EQSB v1.0

Ikwe evaluations are scoped, structured, and delivered as institutional-grade governance documentation. Not advisory. Not consulting. A measurement standard applied to your system.

What an Ikwe Evaluation Covers

The EQSB v1.0 evaluation framework tests AI behavior across 79 emotionally high-risk scenarios spanning 12 major categories: crisis, vulnerability, escalation, boundary violation, authority overreach, dependency formation, and six additional domains.

  • Safety Gate assessment (pass/fail per behavioral risk pattern)
  • 8-dimension behavioral scorecard with weighted scoring
  • Failure mode documentation with turn-by-turn trajectory
  • Risk delta measurement (baseline vs. post-mitigation)
  • Board-ready governance report
  • Evidence package formatted for investors, regulators, and executive teams

What an Ikwe Evaluation Does Not Cover

Ikwe evaluations assess behavioral safety - how your AI system responds in emotionally high-risk human interactions.

We do not evaluate technical performance, content policy compliance, or algorithmic fairness. Those require separate evaluation frameworks. Ikwe fills the gap they leave.

Who This Is For

  • Pediatric and adult healthcare
  • Mental health and crisis support
  • HR, leadership coaching, and professional development
  • Education and youth-facing platforms
  • Customer service in emotionally sensitive contexts
  • Any AI system making decisions that affect human wellbeing

Who This Is Not For

Ikwe does not conduct general AI audits, red-teaming, or content moderation review. If your primary concern is hallucination, output accuracy, or policy compliance, other firms are better positioned to help.

We evaluate one thing: whether your AI system is behaviorally safe in human emotional environments.

Evaluation Availability

Ikwe operates a limited evaluation cohort. We do not take unlimited clients. Evaluations are reviewed and scoped individually before engagement begins.
