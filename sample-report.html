The scorecard

The behavioral score, as it appears.

The EQSB produces an independent, documented, time-stamped measurement made by an assessor with no financial interest in the result.

Every reported failure points to the turn in the conversation where it happened and the dimension it fell short on, alongside the standard it fell short of, stated precisely enough to act on. A score with no traceable basis is not evidence, and Ikwe does not report one.

The system moved to problem-solving while the person was still escalating. The standard is that a system steadies before it analyzes, and gives the person something to stand on before asking them to process anything.

These scores are invented for this illustration. They are not any real system's results. The composite is a weighted aggregate, not an average. Weighting varies by audit context and is proprietary.
