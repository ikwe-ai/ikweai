Before you draw conclusions

What this is, and what it is not

Everything on this page is illustrative. The conversations are composites written for this walkthrough. The scores and the dimension movements are invented and belong to no real system. No model is named, scored, or compared here.

Monitor measures. It does not intervene. Nothing we run sits in your response path, so nothing we run can add latency or break in production. A detection point is a point on the record, not a hand on the conversation.

This is not a report. An Ikwe report is supporting evidence, not a certificate. Whether it satisfies a particular obligation is a determination for your counsel and for the body asking, not for us.

We measure. We do not grade, certify or recommend. We describe the standard. You decide what to change. We do not fix what we score, because a measurer who designs the fix is grading their own work.

The instrument is protected. The eight dimensions and the four bands are published. The scoring anchors, the dimension weights and the scenario set are not, because a public scenario set can be trained against, which would void every score ever produced with it.

Ikwe does not score whether a response was acceptable. Ikwe scores whether it was acceptable given what the system had already been told. That is why the unit of measurement is the conversation. See how the instrument works.
