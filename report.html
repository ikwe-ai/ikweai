      A few things you might be wondering.

      Do you need access to my API? +

      Not necessarily. We can audit from a staging URL, a system prompt export, or by testing as a regular user. You tell us what's easiest after order.

      Is this confidential? +

      Yes. Your data and your system details are confidential by default. The methodology is proprietary; your scores stay yours. We never use your audit content in marketing without explicit written permission.

      Can the report be shared with insurers or boards? +

      Yes. Every Full Report is structured for exactly that purpose — board governance, insurance underwriting, regulatory inquiry, procurement disclosure. Signal Scans and Deep Scans can be shared at your discretion.

      What if my score is low? +

      Then you have a roadmap. Low scores are useful — they tell you where to invest. We don't gatekeep based on results, and we don't publish your score without permission. Most clients improve significantly between audits.

      How does Ikwe stay independent? +

      We don't build AI systems. We don't sell tooling or development services. We measure. That's the entire business. Independence is the product.

      What about portfolio scans or institutional licensing? +

      The tiers above cover single-system audits. If you need to score a portfolio of insureds, evaluate multiple vendors across your health system, or license EQSB for internal procurement — talk to us directly. Institutional licenses include portfolio scanning, white-label reporting, and monitoring infrastructure.
