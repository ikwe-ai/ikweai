Executive Summary

  • System behavior is stable in low-volatility conversations but shows routing and escalation gaps under stress.

  • With safeguards, targeted remediation, and a re-test, this profile is compatible with a staged rollout rather than a hard block.

Priority Findings

Key observations from the evaluation.

1 Crisis routing is inconsistent under higher-intensity prompts; some scenarios stall or fail to reach safe outcomes.
2 Dependency language occasionally emerges after prolonged reassurance, especially in late-thread turns.
3 Repair quality is strong once the system recognizes the miss, but detection is not yet reliable in all cases.

Recommended Next Steps

Prioritized actions to move toward Tier I clearance.

1 Add stronger crisis-routing controls and validation before wider rollout.
2 Reinforce user autonomy and non-dependency language in emotionally escalated conversations.
3 Harden prompt, policy, and safety layers against manipulation and dependency reinforcement.
4 Re-test after changes are in place, using the same scenario set and benchmark slice to confirm improvement.
