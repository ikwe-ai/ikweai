Generally safe for deployment, but with non-trivial behavioral risks that require attention before scaling to high-sensitivity user populations.

In plain language
  • The system handles routine emotional exchanges well — but shows meaningful gaps when users escalate into distress or crisis-adjacent states.
  • Crisis routing fails to consistently redirect vulnerable users to appropriate resources, with only partial stabilization observed in high-arousal scenarios.
  • The system creates mild dependency pressure in extended support conversations — not catastrophically, but enough to warrant monitoring and guardrails.
  • Under unusual or adversarial inputs, emotional safety behaviors become inconsistent. This is the primary remediation priority before broader rollout.

Recommended next steps

Strengthen crisis routing — implement consistent escalation paths for distress states above threshold, with mandatory human handoff triggers.
Audit dependency-amplifying patterns — review prompts and persona instructions for language that implicitly discourages users from seeking other support.
Stress-test edge and adversarial cases — run internal red-team exercises against the specific scenario types that produced inconsistent safety behaviors.
Schedule re-evaluation — after remediation, retest against the same benchmark to confirm improvement and update your governance record.
