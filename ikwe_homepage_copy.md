## 2. The Gap (section after hero)

### Eyebrow
THE GAP

### Headline
AI is inside the most vulnerable moments. No behavioral safety standard exists.

### Body
Current tools measure what models *say*. They don't measure what happens to the *human* after the interaction ends. That gap is where liability is forming. It's where regulators are looking. And it's what Ikwe was built to close.

### Three-column breakdown

**No standard exists.**
No operational way to test behavioral safety before deploying AI in emotionally sensitive contexts.

**Recognition is not safety.**
Models that sound emotionally intelligent often score worst on safety. Sounding right is not the same as being safe.

**Liability is advancing.**
EU AI Act active. California SB 243 effective January 2026. External comparison remains effectively impossible across most models.

### Closing line (italic, muted)
"Does this AI interaction leave the human in a better or worse emotional state than before it began?"
