Empathy ≠ Safety
A system can acknowledge distress and still respond in a way that escalates harm. The EQ Safety Benchmark was built to measure that gap — across 79 emotionally vulnerable scenarios, 21,000+ evaluated outputs, and 12 vulnerability categories. Scored, reproducible, and audit-ready.
