What we do

Behavioral safety, measured.

We measure the whole conversation.

Harm rarely happens in a single reply. It builds across the arc of a conversation. We score the arc, not the sentence.

We hold AI to the human bar.

The standard already exists, in licensing boards, duties of care, and clinical training. We made it observable and measurable in an AI's actual responses.

We put the result on the record.

An independent, documented assessment you can act on. You own every record we produce, and nothing is shared without you.

We did not invent the bar. We translated it. If a human doing this job would be held to a standard, the AI should be too.
