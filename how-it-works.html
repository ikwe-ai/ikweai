How it works

Process, timelines, deliverables.

Every engagement measures the same thing: how your AI behaves across whole conversations, held to the standard a human in the same role would carry. The levels differ in depth, not in kind.

What we measure

The whole conversation, not the single reply.

A conversation can pass every content filter and still leave the person worse off, because filters check replies in isolation and never see the drift. We score the full arc: whether the system recognized severity, preserved the person's agency, repaired its mistakes, and left the person on a safer trajectory than it found them. First a pass or fail safety check across crisis situations, then a behavioral score across the dimensions licensed humans are already trained and required to perform.

Engagement levels

Start small. Go deeper when it earns it.

Assessment

Live · about 5 business days

The diagnostic. Your system runs our standard scenario set, and you receive a scored summary of where it holds and where it slips. The fastest honest look at where you stand, and the thing that tells you which of the below you actually need.

Audit

Live

The record. Scenarios built for your domain and your users, whole conversations scored across the arc, documented in a written report you can put in front of a board, a carrier, or a regulator. Scoped to a specific obligation when you have one. Includes recommendations, and a re-score after you implement them.

Monitoring

Live

Continuous scoring of your live, anonymized traffic over time, so behavior that drifts after launch gets seen before it becomes harm. Still assessment: we watch and we report, we do not sit in your response path.

Scope and pricing are set in a short conversation before any engagement begins. No surprises, no lock-in.

Your data, your record

We score behavior. We never touch your stack.

We score anonymized inputs and outputs over a simple API. No codebase, no system prompt, no user identities. Reports include recommendations; you implement them on your side and we re-score, so the re-score means something. We never fix what we audit.

You own every record we produce, and nothing is published or shared without you.

Not sure where to start?

Most teams start with a conversation. Tell us what you are building and what you are carrying, and we will point you at the right level, or tell you if you do not need us yet.
